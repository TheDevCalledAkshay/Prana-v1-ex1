Our story

Prana is the breath
beneath every moment.

Prana (प्राण) is the Sanskrit word for breath and life force — the quiet energy under everything you do.

We make jewellery for people who want that awareness close at hand: pieces that look contemporary, feel considered, and give you a reason to pause. Not accessories to be saved for occasions, but companions for ordinary days.

i

Intention

Every piece is designed around a single quality to carry, so what you wear says something you mean.

ii

Craft

Natural stones, chosen one by one and strung with care, finished with understated metalwork.

iii

Calm

Clean lines, soft colour and a considered weight. Made to settle you, not to shout.
